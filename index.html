My journey

My journey in technology began with robotics, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and computer vision, where I worked on research and development projects involving intelligent automation, deep learning, IoT, and engineering prototyping. This foundation sparked my interest in building technology that connects data, cloud computing, and AI to solve real-world problems.

I transitioned into Data Engineering at Cognizant, starting as a Data Engineer Intern and progressing to Associate Data Engineer. I worked on scalable data engineering and analytics solutions across Microsoft Azure, building ETL/ELT pipelines using technologies such as Azure Data Factory, Azure Databricks, Azure Synapse Analytics, Apache Spark, PySpark, Python, SQL, and Azure Data Lake Storage.

I later moved into Data Science at Cognizant, expanding my experience into machine learning and predictive analytics on AWS. I worked across the ML lifecycle, including data preparation, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, model development, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring using Amazon SageMaker, scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, pandas, NumPy, AWS Glue, Amazon S3, Athena, and QuickSight.

Today, I work as a Junior Data Engineer at PwC Canada, contributing to enterprise data and AI initiatives and strengthening my experience in building reliable, scalable, and business-focused technology solutions. My interests lie at the intersection of Cloud, Data Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and enterprise technology transformation.

Alongside my technical career, I developed leadership and stakeholder-management experience as Vice-President External of the Engineering and Computer Science Graduate Association (ECSGA) at Concordia University, collaborating with students, university stakeholders, organizations, and external partners to support community engagement and professional-development initiatives.

My academic background includes a Bachelor’s degree from Sreenidhi Institute of Science and Technology and graduate studies in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Concordia University, where I have continued strengthening my expertise in cloud computing, data, AI, and software systems.

I enjoy continuous learning, solving complex technical problems, and exploring how Cloud, Data, and AI can be used to build scalable and impactful solutions. I’m always interested in connecting with professionals across Data Engineering, Data Science, AI/ML, and Cloud Computing.
